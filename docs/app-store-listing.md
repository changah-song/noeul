### English (U.S.)

- **Name:** Noeul
- **Subtitle:** Learn a language by reading
- **Promotional text:** Read real books in the language you're learning. Tap any word for its meaning and roots, or any sentence for a translation, without leaving the page.
- **Keywords:** korean,reading,learn korean,hanja,chinese,english,dictionary,vocabulary,flashcards,epub,graded
- **Description:**

  Noeul is a reading app for people learning a language by reading real books, not doing drills.

  Import your own EPUB or PDF, or download a book from the built-in public-domain library, and start reading in your target language. Tap a word for an instant dictionary lookup; hold a sentence to translate it.

  • Instant lookups: meaning, pronunciation and examples without leaving the page
  • Character roots: Korean and Chinese words broken into their Hanja / Hanzi, with related words
  • Meaning in context: AI explains what a word means in this exact sentence, including literary uses
  • Fits your level: Noeul learns which words you know and highlights only the ones you probably don't
  • Saved words: review with spaced-repetition flashcards
  • Listen mode: have the book read aloud
  • Comfortable reading: paged or vertical scroll, adjustable type, bookmarks and notes

  Supports reading in Korean, English and Chinese. Your books, words and progress stay on your device, with optional sync when you sign in.
